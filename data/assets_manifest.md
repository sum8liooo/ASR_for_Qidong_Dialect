## Key numbers — viva quick-reference

**Zero-shot vs full-corpus (test, n=470):**

| Metric | Zero-shot | Full | Absolute ↓ | Relative ↓ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Corpus CER | 0.8056 | 0.5886 | 0.2170 | **26.9 %** |
| Sentence CER | 0.7963 | 0.5983 | 0.1980 | 24.9 % |
| Word-list CER | 0.9476 | 0.4410 | 0.5066 | **53.5 %** |

**Reproducibility (0.1 h, seeds {42,1,2}):**
- Corpus CER = **0.7451 ± 0.0569** (range 0.1104)
- Sentence CER = 0.7374 ± 0.0612
- Word-list CER = 0.8617 ± 0.0126 (≈4.5× more stable than corpus)
- ⚠️ SD 0.057 exceeds the 0.026 gap between the 0.25 h and 0.5 h tiers → intermediate-tier ranking is within run-to-run noise; only the coarse monotone trend is claimed.

**Per-speaker CER (full):** spk01 = 0.578, spk08 = 0.5202 (Δ = 0.0578 ≈ same size as the seed variance).

**Model / compute:**
- Trainable params: **3,932,160 (3.93 M) / 1,547,422,720 total = 0.2541 %** (LoRA r=8, q_proj+v_proj).
- Full-corpus training: 17,465 s ≈ **4.85 h** wall-clock (batch 1, grad-accum 16, grad-ckpt, 4-bit QLoRA).
- **VRAM peak ≈ 4.9 GB** — fits the 12 GB RTX 4070 Ti with wide headroom.
- Total compute: ~17 h (5-tier sweep) + ~6.6 h (3 reproducibility seeds).

**Corpus:** 11 speakers, 2277 utterances, **1.36 h** after silence-trim. Speaker-independent split — test: spk01/spk08; val: spk03/spk09; train: other 7 speakers = **0.813 h** (1324 utts). 13 utterances skipped as untranscribable (listed in Table A.3).
